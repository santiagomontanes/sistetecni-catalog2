# Surface brief: home (src/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: students and people in Colombia, on a phone, coming from Instagram/Facebook. Primary action: see the catalog. Secondary: WhatsApp. Social video is part of the page.

Constraints: design only. Every existing function (Supabase loading, ProductCard routes, hero media from admin, WhatsApp, admin, /tarjeta) stays. Additions allowed. Keep logo and #1A56DB. Avoid: looks like another brand, heavy or slow on mobile, template/AI look, too serious.

Latest-video embeds: official Instagram/Facebook embeds; post links live in src/config/social-feed.ts and are updated by hand.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is Sistetecni's dispatch table: every laptop is a parcel ready to ship. It refuses the generic tech-store arrangement of hero photo, icon-card row and product grid.
OWN-WORLD: Drenched brand blue (#1A56DB) like a courier poly mailer, white thermal shipping labels with barcode, ink #0B1020, one hazard-yellow #FFD400 handling sticker reserved for the 8-month warranty; condensed bold sans for labels and headlines, a data face only for prices and codes; packing-tape bands; tracking-status steps.
STORY: The visitor understands in seconds that these are verified laptops with real warranty and national shipping, believes it because a real person and shop show up in the latest videos, and taps "Ver catálogo" (or WhatsApp).
